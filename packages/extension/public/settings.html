Steplight privacy settings

Everything stays on this device. These settings decide how much of a browsing session Steplight keeps. Some settings are managed by your organization.

Skip sensitive sites?

Recording is rarely a good idea on banking sites, health portals and password managers. You can add a suggested list of such sites to your deny list; nothing is recorded on denied sites.

What is captured

Always on, whatever you choose: password fields, payment-card and one-time-code fields, and fields named like card / CVV / OTP / PIN / SSN / Aadhaar / PAN are never read, and anything editable inside a payment provider's frame. Secrets (emails, cards, API keys, tokens…) are redacted before they leave the page.
